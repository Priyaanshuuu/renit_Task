# Future scope

My next priority would be to confirm which decision is still difficult for the buyer after using the pilot, then make the smallest change that improves it.

## First improvements I would explore

1. Confirm the operational cutoff and delivery-day convention with the client.
2. Extend the historical period to check seasonal performance.
3. Automate updates and show freshness warnings if the portal becomes a daily tool.
4. Compare forecast accuracy at different decision times on common matched periods.
5. Evaluate historical uncertainty ranges when enough earlier data is available.

## Work that needs a wider data scope

Customer demand, purchase contracts, and renewable generation allocation would be needed to estimate the client's remaining electricity requirement. Price data and explicit commercial assumptions would be needed to study financial exposure.

I would also consider other renewable sources, saved scenarios, accounts, and a decision audit trail if the client needs them. Those additions go beyond the two Elexon wind datasets allowed in this pilot and would need an agreed scope extension.

The deferred engineering options are in [the advanced approach](03-advanced-approach.md). I would validate each proposed improvement with the people using the portal before adding infrastructure.
